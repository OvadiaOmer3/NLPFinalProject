9. Windowed vs static strain
==============================================================================
windowed on top of static   chi2(2)=2.1 p=3.56e-01
static on top of windowed   chi2(2)=2.6 p=2.68e-01

Neither adds to the other: ~90% of the 19,384-entity universe is active
in every 50,000-step window, so windowed strain is a near-copy of static.
NOTE this is SATURATION -- a different mechanism from the uniform-sampling
argument that retired the windowed EXPOSURE term (section 5).
